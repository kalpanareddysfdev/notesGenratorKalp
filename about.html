About Kalpana's Learning Hub

Kalpana's Learning Hub is a personal collection of study materials and browser-based tools for exploring technical topics and organizing practical decisions.

What you'll find here

  • A study-note generator for drafting structured notes from a topic or source text.
  • Reference PDFs covering cloud fundamentals, AI tools, and study workflows.
  • A PG property report tool for exploring costs, occupancy, and return assumptions.

Generated study material can be incomplete or inaccurate, so check important facts against dependable sources. Calculations are estimates based on the values entered and are not financial advice.
